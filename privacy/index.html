Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Kata – Sudoku & Logic Puzzles does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send anything about you anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your boards in all eight games (Sudoku, Tenbin, Light Up, Star Battle, Killer Sudoku, Pipes, Ink Levels and Nonogram), progress, statistics, settings and everything else Kata remembers are stored in the app's own files on your phone. They are never uploaded. Kata does not use iCloud yet, so nothing is synced between your devices. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it.

The camera

"From a book" can read a printed sudoku with the camera. The digits are read on your phone. The picture is not saved and is not sent anywhere. Kata asks for camera access only when you open that screen, and only uses the camera while it is open. You can also type the board in, or choose a photo.

Photos

If you share a solved sudoku as a card and choose "Save Image", Kata adds that picture to your photo library. It never reads your library.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. It is a notification made on your phone. No server is involved.

Purchases

Kata Pro is sold through the App Store, paid once or as a yearly subscription. Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. We can only see, on your phone, that a purchase is active.

No tracking, no third parties

Kata contains no advertising, no analytics and no code from other companies. It does not track you across other apps or websites. It has no leaderboards and does not use Game Center, so your results are not shared with anyone.

Children

Kata is a puzzle game for everyone. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing about a child to collect.

Your rights

Because Kata holds no personal data about you outside your own phone, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove everything Kata stores on your phone, delete the app.

If you write to us

When you email us, we get your email address and what you write. Send feedback in the app opens an email in your own mail app, with the app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model filled in at the bottom so we can find a problem; you see all of it and can change it before you send. The app itself sends nothing. We use what you write only to answer you. It is not shared with anyone, and we delete it when the matter is closed. Ask us to delete it sooner and we will.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes your IP address in order to deliver the pages. The fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new version will be on this page before the app changes, with a new date at the top.

Contact
