Support

Something not working, or a question about Kata – Sudoku & Logic Puzzles?

In the app, tap Feedback at the bottom of You, or Send feedback in Settings, or write to madebyslab@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and which iOS version you have, which game you were playing, and what you were doing. A screenshot helps.

Restore a purchase

Tap the person icon at the top right of Play or Learn to open You, tap the gear at the top right, then Restore purchases, under Kata Pro. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Kata Pro also comes back on a new phone this way.

Refunds

Kata Pro is either paid once, for life, or a yearly subscription. Cancel the yearly one in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Kata Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the year you paid for. Paid once has nothing to cancel. With the yearly one, You > gear also has Manage subscription. Apple handles refunds; Kata cannot give one. Ask for it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is free and what is Pro

Sudoku on all six levels, Tenbin, Light Up and Star Battle are free, with hints and the daily boards, including the past week of daily boards under Earlier days. So are the lessons and drills for the first twelve sudoku techniques and the first two in Tenbin, Light Up and Star Battle. Kata Pro adds Killer Sudoku, Pipes, Ink Levels and Nonogram. One of them is free to try each week. On Play their cards carry the Pro seal, and a tap shows what Pro is. Pro also adds the lessons and drills for the nine advanced sudoku techniques and the harder ones in Killer Sudoku, Tenbin, Light Up and Star Battle, and the review of a solved board, where you look back at how you solved it. Pipes, Ink Levels and Nonogram have no lessons or reviews. Reviews are kept for your 30 most recent boards in each game, and for daily boards for 90 days.

Where are the rules?

Tap ⋯ at the top right of a game's card on Play, or on a board, and choose How to play: the rules with small examples. The first time you open a game, a short demo starts where you make the moves yourself. You can skip it. Strategies, under the same ⋯ on a game's card, and under Learn, lists the techniques of Sudoku, Killer Sudoku, Tenbin, Light Up and Star Battle. Pipes, Ink Levels and Nonogram have no strategies page.

The clock

Under You, gear, Clock is a switch. With the clock off there is no clock, and hints whenever you like. With it on you get a clock, a limit on hints per board, lives for wrong entries and, if you want one, a time limit, all set in the lines below the switch. The clock is on for new players. It applies to every game, and a board you have started keeps the limits it started with.

The camera read my board wrong

"From a book" is under ⋯ on the Sudoku card on Play. Hold the phone square above the page, in good light, so the grid fills the frame. Digits that Kata is unsure of are marked, and you check them against the page before you play. You can always fix a digit by hand, or type the whole board in. "Choose a photo" works with any photo of a sudoku.

The daily reminder

It is off by default. Turn it on under You, gear, Daily reminder. It only comes on days you have not played yet. If it does not arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Kata in the iPhone's Settings app.

My progress

Everything is stored on your phone, and Kata has no account and does not use iCloud yet. If you delete the app, or get a new phone without restoring a backup, progress does not come along. Purchases do: use Restore purchases.

Does Kata need the internet?

No. Boards are made on your phone, and the daily boards come with the app or are worked out on the phone, the same for everyone. The only things that need a connection are buying Kata Pro and restoring a purchase.

A board that cannot be solved, or a hint that is wrong

Boards in Kata are made to have exactly one solution, found by logic. Each step in a hint is worked out from what is on the board. If you think you have found a mistake, please write. Tell us the game, the level and the date (or the technique), and what you had placed. A screenshot is the easiest way.

A board from a book that needs a technique Kata doesn't know yet opens as Off the scale. The hints help as far as Kata's twenty-one techniques reach, and then say so.
